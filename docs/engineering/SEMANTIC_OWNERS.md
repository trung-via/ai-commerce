# AI Commerce — Semantic ownership (A01 registry)

This defines which *product* domain owns a fact. It does not require an agent, DB or separate microservice per owner.

| Domain | Owns | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Commerce Opportunity | Trend hypotheses, audience/opportunity and own-brand decision briefs | Fact that a trend will go viral or capital is authorized |
| Product Intelligence | Product identity, verified/uncertain claims, offers and rights evidence | Live permission to publish on a platform |
| Media & Creative | Concepts, scripts, media assets and creative hypothesis meaning | External commercial order or settled income |
| Distribution | Account/placement rights, publication states, links and raw platform metrics | Settled finance or creative causality |
| Commerce Operations | Product job state, asset custody, actual costs and settlement reconciliation | Permissions for another provider/account |

`Engineering Foundation` owns software engineering workflow and code delivery, **not a sixth semantic truth store**. Human remains highest decision authority. EF01/EF02 must not import AIOS Operator, shared credentials, cost ledger or any historical AIOS TASK/REVIEW/PASS state.
