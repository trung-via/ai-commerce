## Interpretation (not additional Human constraints)
- **Internal rule override is unlimited.** Any internal workflow, budgets, task gate, quality rubric, schema or instruction can be changed by Human.
- **External rights are facts, not project approvals.** Human cannot make a nonexistent platform entitlement, license or provider receipt materialize by rewriting governance; normal evidence can identify these as absent, even if Human chooses a different internal workflow.
- **No silent false success claim.** Simplified reporting may state "unverified"; the system should not claim a video was actually public/paid when it lacks evidence.
- **No default creative censorship.** Advisory QA is available when chosen; it is not a mandatory design or editorial committee.
