## Chuẩn bị trước khi mời hoặc cho xem bất kỳ media nào

1. Preregister the study question, target population, inclusion/exclusion
   category labels, applicable rubric/version, primary pair-preference endpoint,
   secondary disagreement and position association, and exact protocol/asset
   SHA pins. Fiction, animation, silent media and product_ref=null are valid
   design choices. Declared applicability is not inspected quality or rights.
2. Obtain independent statistician review of effect size/precision/power,
   analysis units, rater/asset clustering, exclusions, attrition and stopping
   rules. Sample size and power are **PENDING**, never inferred from synthetic
   reviewer slots. No 24/36/48-rater enrollment or adequate sample is asserted.
   Freeze the design before outcomes; record deviations before interpretation.
3. Review rights provenance and authorized use for the exact asset bytes,
   likeness, voice, product/logo and intended media exposure. Review sensitive
   content and safety. Unverified rights or unsafe exposure -> HOLD.
4. Mỗi người tham gia phải tự opt-in qua dịch vụ riêng được cấp quyền trước
   khi phân công phiên, phơi lộ media hoặc ghi nhận câu trả lời. Thông báo rõ
   mục đích, cách dùng dữ liệu, tính tự nguyện, quyền rút lui, quyền truy cập,
   thời hạn lưu/xóa, điều kiện thù lao (nếu có) và đầu mối tổ chức qua kênh
   được phê duyệt. Không thu tên/email/ngân hàng trong source hoặc test.
   Consent phải gắn phiên bản privacy notice, phạm vi, thời điểm hợp lệ trước
   exposure và trạng thái revocation. Chat của organizer không thay consent.
5. Approve a restricted external custodian for necessary identity/consent
   linkage, access, retention and deletion. Keep only study-scoped pseudonyms
   in a separately authorized future analysis sidecar. Minimize categories;
   do not derive sensitive traits or identities from media. No raw submission,
   raw-PII hash, identity log or private file path belongs in this source cut.

## Independent evidence required before any observation could be admitted

The separate authorized consent service must provide participant-specific
informed opt-in, privacy notice/version, scope and revocation state valid
before exposure. Independent rights evidence must authorize the exact SHA and
exposure use. A separate verifier must attest pseudonym, session assignment,
actual exposure time and task, keeping the private mapping out of public UI.
Response/withdrawal validation must bind the protocol, exact asset SHA, blinded
task digest, one-use nonce, replay resistance and expiry. Independent signer
custody, public-key chain, key revocation and trusted verification time are
required. A project Windows SID does not attest participant identity.

No such service, signer, key chain, receipt intake or authenticated verifier
is connected or authorized by BQ-03b. Caller JSON, consent=true, allow_true,
signed-looking strings, fake callbacks and claims of withdrawal remain
UNVERIFIED / NOT_ADMITTED / HOLD. Closed request references are untrusted
metadata, never proof. Revoked, expired, replayed, unsigned, wrong-protocol,
wrong-asset, missing or invalid-scope references retain explicit reason codes.
Unknown effects remain UNKNOWN with no automatic retry. Future admissible
observations require another separately versioned authenticated module and
separate Human, participant, rights and privacy authorization.

## Planned blinding and analysis

Keep native BQ-03a seed, private A/B mapping, source/file references and asset
SHA pins private. Use only BQ03bPublicProtocolPreview.v1 for public protocol
preview. The preview is design text; no blind UI or actual exposure platform is
deployed. BQ-03a's intended HMAC assignment is synthetic replay material.
Future actual allocation needs independent randomization, masked model/provider
and asset labels, and between-person AB/BA counterbalance. Repeated ratings
within a person and assets across persons require clustering. Equal planned
arms do not establish equal usable responses or independent raters.

Preregister rater selection/context bias, position/order effects, fatigue,
carryover and blindness-breach checks. Use within-person crossover only with a
separately reviewed order/washout analysis. Preserve missingness, ties,
abstentions and dropout counts with explicit denominators. Missing is not zero;
ties are a separate agreement category; ties/abstentions/missing are excluded
from binary preference intervals. Unadjusted synthetic Wilson/Fleiss statistics
remain descriptive, not Human calibration or sampling validity.

## Rút lui và đóng readiness

Trước exposure phải có đường rút lui độc lập và quyền thu hồi consent rõ ràng.
Sau yêu cầu rút lui, dừng exposure/thu dữ liệu, xác thực qua dịch vụ được phép,
loại dữ liệu đã thu hồi khỏi phân tích theo chính sách đã duyệt, tính lại kết
quả và thực hiện retention/deletion có audit tối thiểu. Một người tự nhận là
withdrawer không được quyền sửa/xóa dữ liệu người khác. BQ-03b không nhận
identity, không ghi nhận withdrawal thật và không xóa dữ liệu.

Readiness checklist requires authentic consent, exposure rights, independent
verifier/key custody, withdrawal path, approved retention/deletion/access,
blinding platform, preregistered sample/power, safety, revocation/expiry and
privacy-minimized audit logs. Missing evidence -> NOT_READY_FOR_LIVE_STUDY /
AUTHENTIC_HUMAN_DATA_NOT_AVAILABLE. Brain may review the draft but cannot
automatically admit data, approve a quality gate or replace participant consent.
Q2 P0, Q4 UNKNOWN and T3 NOT_RUN remain unchanged. Preference does not prove
rights, factual correctness, naturalness, ROI or virality.
