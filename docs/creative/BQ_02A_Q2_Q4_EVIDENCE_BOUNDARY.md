The decision order is P0/A13 FAIL → HOLD, then P1 → REVISE, then blocking UNKNOWN → UNKNOWN, otherwise PASS_PREFLIGHT_ADVISORY. Expected pre-render uncertainty is not a measured defect. PASS_PREFLIGHT_ADVISORY remains only a plan advisory after T1 succeeds. Source provenance UNKNOWN, audio UNKNOWN and an explicitly UNKNOWN constraint are blocking uncertainty. Optional supplied voiced/acoustic direction retains UNPROVEN rights; it cannot grant permission. A product reference alone does not establish product truth or license.

`product_ref=null` requires no invented commercial facts. Silent footage requires no lip sync or speech quality; absent measured audio is NOT_APPLICABLE. Voiceover requires no on-screen lip sync. FICTION, STYLIZED, ANIMATION and INTENTIONAL_FANTASY do not imply failed physics. An animation of a flying cup can be intentional. Cross-shot endpoint questions still refer to symbolic plan continuity only. Black and silence detectors report intervals, never an automatic creative P0.

T1_DETERMINISTIC uses the unchanged authoritative `affiliate_release/inspection.py`, MediaReleaseInspection.v1, inspector `a13-c08/1.0`. The bridge delegates all local safe-byte access, hashing, tool execution, full decode, metadata and diagnostics to `inspect_media_release`; it contains no ffprobe/FFmpeg implementation and no second custody ledger. Tool versions, measured stream facts, expected/observed SHA, full decode and complete nested record are retained. A report FAIL/UNKNOWN retains reasons and cannot become verified. A13 diagnostics UNKNOWN also leaves the requested technical coverage UNKNOWN. No asset yields NOT_INSPECTED and PRE_RENDER_ONLY, with no fabricated SHA, timestamp or spatial region.

The public evidence ingress is explicit `Path root` plus native A13 assets/options/limits. Hand-entered findings have no observed authority. Native projection and review validate the nested A13 record and recompute every field. Serialized asset-bearing evidence/review requires explicit local A13 reinspection and exact equality; a plausible dict, recomputed hash or manually constructed A13 object alone cannot create native OBSERVED evidence through the public API. The in-memory binding is not a cryptographic signature or persistent attestation against hostile Python execution. Portable authenticity requires independent local reinspection. This distinguishes actual tool invocation from imagined findings.

T2_NOT_RUN means no OCR/ASR, pronunciation/lipsync, motion/identity or other multimodal detector has run. T3_NOT_RUN means no calibrated human/perceptual evaluation has run. The Q2 post-inspection challenge can reason about genuinely measured A13 facts and diagnostics only. It cannot say an object moves incorrectly, a person is angry, Vietnamese pronunciation is wrong, or a brand is present. No detector confidence, human quality measurement, conversion/ROI prediction or source truth follows from technical metadata. T1 observations stay separate from plan hypotheses, and no record grants external effects or publication.

All complete nested BQ-02a records are bounded to 512 KiB of canonical UTF-8, depth 24, 32768 JSON nodes, 4096 members per collection, 16 KiB per string and finite numbers within ±(2^53−1). Caller notes are at most 16, each text at most 2048 UTF-8 bytes, with exact source/shot refs. Duplicate JSON keys/IDs, extra fields, cycles, invalid Unicode and wrong digest pins reject. Existing BQ-01a/BQ-01b records retain their stricter native bounds; A13 retains its existing resource ceilings. A valid but too large source combination also rejects; no truncation manufactures missing evidence. Structured source claims drive critical holds; no NLP, OCR or hidden fact extraction is claimed over free text. Caller questions retain their text as untrusted data and never establish observations.

Qualified module imports expose `critic.preflight_critique(plan, plan_digest=...)`, `inspection_evidence.inspect_local_t1(Path(root), [ReleaseAsset(...)], diagnostics=..., limits=...)` and `quality_review.adversarial_review(preflight, preflight_digest=..., technical_evidence=..., technical_evidence_digest=...)`. The latter two digest pins are distinct. Omitting asset evidence produces an explicit NOT_INSPECTED receipt; omitting direction includes no BQ-01b draft. Native records are frozen canonical values with detached dictionaries. `from_json(..., root=Path(...))` replays serialized asset evidence/reviews through actual local A13 inspection; pre-render-only JSON replay is pure. This is an advisory source API, with no new agent, provider selection, implicit search, strategy rewrite or runtime registration.
